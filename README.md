# ⚖️ LawYaar — Your Friendly Legal Guide

LawYaar is a simple and user-friendly legal awareness platform designed to help common citizens understand their basic legal rights, laws, and everyday legal situations in easy language.

The main goal of LawYaar is to make legal awareness accessible to everyone without complicated legal jargon.

## 📌 About The Project

Many people are unaware of their basic legal rights and do not know where to find simple and reliable legal information.

LawYaar aims to solve this problem by providing:

- Simple explanations of legal rights
- Beginner-friendly legal guides
- Common legal awareness topics
- Emergency support information
- Frequently Asked Questions
- Interactive legal awareness quiz
- Search functionality for legal topics

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Dashboard
A central dashboard that provides quick access to important legal awareness sections.

### ⚖️ Know Your Rights
Learn about basic rights and protections available to citizens.

### 📚 Legal Guides
Step-by-step beginner-friendly guides for common legal situations such as:

- Consumer complaints
- Online scams
- Police-related concerns
- Property documents

### 🚨 Emergency Help
Provides quick awareness about what to do during an immediate emergency and where to seek appropriate support.

### ❓ FAQs
Answers common questions about LawYaar and legal awareness.

### 🧠 Legal Quiz
An interactive quiz that helps users test their basic understanding of legal awareness.

### 🔎 Search
Search for relevant rights, laws, and legal guides.

### 🌙 Dark Mode
User-friendly interface with light and dark mode support.

### 📱 Responsive Design
Designed to work across desktop and mobile screen sizes.
