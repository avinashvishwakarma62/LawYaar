FIRST RESPONSE

What should you do first?

Simple steps to follow when a situation becomes urgent.

01

Stay safe

Move to a safer location if possible and avoid putting yourself at further risk.

02

Contact appropriate help

Contact the relevant emergency service or authority depending on the situation.

03

Share important information

Clearly communicate your location, situation and other relevant details.

04

Preserve evidence

When safe and appropriate, keep relevant messages, documents, screenshots or other records.

✓
SAFETY FIRST

Your immediate safety comes first.

Legal information can help you understand your options, but during an emergency, contact the appropriate emergency service before trying to resolve the legal issue yourself.
